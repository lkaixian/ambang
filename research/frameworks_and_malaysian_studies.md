## Frameworks: how capacity factors are defined

### Takeaway
No sourced findings obtained.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- UNVERIFIED (memory): Cifuentes (1992) computes PCC = area / area-per-visitor x rotations per day. RCC = PCC x correction factors (CF), each CF = 1 - (limiting magnitude / maximum magnitude). ECC = RCC x management capacity (MC). Typical CFs are social, erosion, accessibility, rainfall/flood, sun, wildlife disturbance, closures. Needs a primary source before use.
- UNVERIFIED (memory): PAP/RAC (UNEP/MAP) 1997 guidelines and UNWTO treat TCC as physical-ecological, socio-demographic, political-economic and infrastructure/facility components. LAC (Stankey 1985) and VERP (US NPS) are indicator-and-standard frameworks with monitoring, not formula-based. TOMM (Manidis Roberts, 1996) is an indicator-based monitoring model.
- UNVERIFIED (memory): The marine/dive literature often cites Hawkins and Roberts (1997), who gave about 5,000-6,000 dives per site per year (Red Sea) as a threshold, and Zakai and Chadwick-Furman (2002; Eilat, 5,000 dives per site per year). Prieto (Dive-site capacity papers) also exist. Verify all.
